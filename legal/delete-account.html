Delete your Amble account

Last updated: 24 September 2026

You can delete your Amble account and everything we store at any time. There are two ways to do it.

In the app

  1. Open Amble and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete account and cloud data.
  3. Confirm twice. The second confirmation is final.

Deletion happens immediately. You do not need to contact us.

By email

If you no longer have the app installed, email privacy@amble.fit from the address you signed up with, asking us to delete your account. We will confirm once it is done, normally within a few working days and always within 30 days.

What gets deleted

Everything we hold that is linked to your account:

  • Your account and sign-in
  • Walks backed up to the cloud, including GPS traces
  • Photos you attached to walks
  • Dog profiles, saved places and saved routes
  • Your preferences and settings
  • Your push notification token, so reminders stop

This is permanent. We do not keep a copy and we cannot restore it afterwards.

What stays on your phone

Amble keeps your walks on your device as well as in the cloud. Deleting your account removes the cloud copy, not the copy on your phone. To remove that too, delete the app. You can export your walks as GPX files first if you want to keep them.

How long it takes

Deletion from our live database is immediate. Encrypted backups are rotated out within 30 days, after which no copy remains anywhere.

Questions

Email privacy@amble.fit. If you are in the UK or EEA you also have the right to complain to a data protection regulator. See our Privacy Policy for details.
