Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 September 2026

This policy describes what Amble does with your data. It's written in plain language because that's how privacy policies should be written. If anything here is unclear, email privacy@amble.fit and we'll fix it.

The short version

  • Amble runs almost entirely on your device. Your walks live on your phone.
  • We don't have a tracking pixel, an analytics SDK, or an advertising network.
  • We don't sell, rent, or share your data. There is no business model that involves your data.
  • Some features need to send anonymous queries to public mapping services (described below). These services see a coordinate or a search term, but they don't know who you are.
  • If you sign in (optional), your saved walks back up to a cloud database we run. That's the only time your data leaves your device tied to your account.

What data Amble handles

On your device, never sent anywhere

  • Your location, while the app is open or you're on an active walk
  • The walks you've completed — distance, time, GPS trace, photos
  • The walks you've saved
  • Your dog profiles
  • Your preferences — duration, terrain, dog mode, pocket mode, taste profile
  • Your reminder time, if you set one

This data is stored on your iPhone in the app's local storage. It does not leave your phone unless one of the following sections applies.

Sent anonymously to public mapping services

To plot you a route, we send small, anonymous queries to public OpenStreetMap-based services. These services do not know you are an Amble user.

ServiceWhat we sendWhy
Overpass API (overpass-api.de) A bounding box around you (typically 1-2 km radius) To download the walking-path data for your area
OpenStreetMap Nominatim A search string (e.g. "Sheffield Cathedral") or coordinate When you type into the via-point picker, or to name your start location
OpenTopoData / SRTM A list of route coordinates To compute the elevation profile of a generated walk
open-meteo.com Your latitude and longitude To show you the current weather glance
BRouter / OpenRouteService / GraphHopper Route start and end coordinates When the offline planner can't compute a route and we fall back to a hosted routing service

These services have their own privacy policies; see Third-party services below. None receive any identifier that links the request to you, your device, or your phone number.

Sent to our cloud (only when signed in)

Sign-in is optional. When you sign in, Amble will:

  • Create an account using your Apple ID or Google account (we receive: a user ID, optionally your name and email if you choose to share them)
  • Back up your saved walks, dog profiles, preferences, and personal POIs to a database we run on Supabase, so you can restore them on a new phone
  • Optionally back up walk photos to our private cloud storage (photos are not publicly accessible)
  • Register a push notification token for your device (so we can send walk reminder notifications you've opted into). This token is tied to your account and deleted when you sign out.
  • Store a stable device identifier alongside your push token to handle token rotation on reinstall. This identifier is not used for tracking or advertising.

The cloud copy is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest (Supabase encrypts its disks). You can delete your account at any time from the Profile tab; deleting wipes everything we hold within 30 days.

Apple Health and Google Health Connect

If you grant permission, Amble can:

  • Read your past walking workouts to populate your Amble history
  • Write each finished Amble walk back to Health as a workout so it counts toward your activity rings

This data flows directly between Amble and the iOS Health / Android Health Connect system. It is not transmitted to our servers. You can revoke this permission at any time in your device settings.

Web planner (amble.fit/plan)

If you use the web trip planner, it stores your sign-in session in your browser's local storage so you stay logged in. No cookies are used. Route planning queries are sent to BRouter (an open-source routing service) over HTTPS; these contain route coordinates but no account information.

Cookies, analytics, advertising

Amble does not use any of these. There is no tracking SDK in the app. We don't measure how often you open the app, which features you use, or how long your sessions last. We do not show ads.

Children's privacy

Amble is rated for general audiences, but it is designed for adults and it collects precise location. It is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in parts of the EEA and UK where that is the applicable age of digital consent), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Children can of course walk with Amble — a parent or guardian should set it up and remain responsible for the walk. Nothing in the app is intended to be used by a child on their own.

If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, email privacy@amble.fit and we will delete it promptly.

Your rights

You can:

  • View, export, and delete every walk Amble has saved, from inside the app
  • Delete your cloud backup (when sign-in is enabled) by signing out and tapping "Delete account"
  • Email us at privacy@amble.fit for any data-related request

If you are in the UK or EEA

Amble is operated from the United Kingdom. We are the data controller for the limited personal data described in this policy, and you can reach us at privacy@amble.fit.

Under the UK GDPR and EU GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete and port your data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. Our lawful bases are: consent for location access, health data and notifications (withdraw it any time in your device settings), and legitimate interests for running and securing the app. We respond to requests within one month.

If you think we have handled your data badly, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) or to your local EEA supervisory authority.

If you are in California

Under the CCPA/CPRA you have the right to know what personal information we collect, to delete it, to correct it, and to opt out of its sale or sharing. We do not sell or share personal information, and we never have. We do not use it for cross-context behavioural advertising. Exercising these rights will never result in worse service. Email privacy@amble.fit.

Everywhere else

We apply the same standard to everyone, wherever you live: you can see, export and delete everything Amble holds, from inside the app. If your country gives you additional rights, email us and we will honour them.

Third-party services

Amble uses these external services. Each has its own privacy policy; the terms apply when your device communicates with them.

  • OpenStreetMap — map data
  • Overpass API — OSM querying
  • OpenFreeMap — map tiles
  • MapLibre — rendering library (no network calls of its own)
  • open-meteo.com — weather
  • OpenTopoData — elevation
  • BRouter — routing fallback
  • OpenRouteService — routing fallback
  • GraphHopper — routing fallback
  • Supabase — cloud backup (only when signed in)
  • Apple HealthKit — when you grant permission
  • Photon (Komoot) — address search. Receives the text you type and, for nearby results, an approximate location.
  • OSRM demo server — estimated drive times to walk destinations. Receives the coordinates being compared. See the note below on “Between us”.
  • RevenueCat — subscription and purchase status, if you ever buy Amble Pro.
  • Google Health Connect — Android only, when you grant permission.

Where these services are located

Amble is operated from the United Kingdom. Several of the services above are run by volunteers, non-profits or companies based elsewhere, including in the European Economic Area and the United States. When your device contacts them, the request — which may include coordinates — leaves your country.

These are direct connections from your device to that service. We do not route them through our own servers, and no account identifier, device identifier or phone number is sent with them. Our own cloud backup (Supabase) is separate and described above.

Where personal data is transferred out of the UK or EEA, we rely on the UK and European Commission adequacy decisions where they apply, and otherwise on the International Data Transfer Agreement or Standard Contractual Clauses. For detail on a specific service, email privacy@amble.fit.

“Between us” — a note about other people’s locations

The “Between us” feature finds a fair meeting point between you and up to four other people. To do that, the places you enter are sent to the OSRM routing service listed above so driving times can be compared.

Enter a town, postcode or general area for someone else rather than their exact home address, unless they are happy for you to. We do not store these locations — they are used to calculate the meeting point and discarded when you close the search. But the request does reach a third-party service, and the people you enter have not agreed to this policy.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top and, if the change is material, show you a notice in the app the next time you open it.
